07 — Questions

07Before you come

Questions

Quick answers. Anything else — WhatsApp the store.

Where exactly is the store?

Shop No 13, Triveni Plaza, Plot B/77, Sector 23, Seawoods East, Navi Mumbai 400706 — near Seawoods railway station, opposite Seawoods Residency. Get directions.

What are the timings?

Open every day, 11 AM to 11 PM.

What do you sell?

Streetwear for Gen-Z: boxy fit shirts, checks, linen shirts, baggy fit jeans, tees, cargos and new drops every few weeks. New drops are posted on Instagram.

How much do things cost?

Prices aren't listed online because they change with every drop. Message the store on WhatsApp for current prices.

Which sizes do you have?

Recent drops: boxy fit shirts in M–XL, linen shirts in M–XXL and baggy fit jeans in 28–36. Stock moves fast, so ask on WhatsApp before you come.

Do you ship outside Navi Mumbai?

Yes, the store ships across India. Message on WhatsApp with the fit you want — the store will confirm size, price, delivery charges and timing.

How can I pay?

Debit cards and tap-to-pay (NFC) mobile payments are accepted. For other options, ask the store.

Can I order on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Yes — visit the shop, DM @thebombaedrip, or WhatsApp 98679 37125 / 70398 74248.
